## 🏎️ The Lamborghini Centenario

The **Lamborghini Centenario** is a futuristic "Few-Off" limited edition hypercar engineered to celebrate the centenary of founder Ferruccio Lamborghini. With only 40 units ever produced, it stands as one of the most exclusive automotive designs in existence.

### ⚡ Key Highlights
* **The V12 Pinnacle:** Equipped with a 6.5L naturally aspirated V12 pushing **770 HP**, launching from 0–100 km/h in a blistering **2.8 seconds** with a top speed exceeding **350 km/h**.
* **Aerodynamic Weapon:** Features a radical, bumper-less rear design dominated by a massive six-fin carbon fiber diffuser and an active, extending rear wing.
* **Rolling Laboratory:** Pioneered Lamborghini’s first-ever rear-wheel steering system and a jet-inspired cockpit with advanced track telemetry tracking.
